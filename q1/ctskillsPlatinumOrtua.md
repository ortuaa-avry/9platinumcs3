## Step 1: Identify the Big Problem
### Main Problem
The PSHS school canteen is small and often gets crowded during lunch break.
---
## Step 2: Identify the Sub-Problems
1. Some students take too long to decide what to order.
2. The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
3. There is no system to track which food items are running out.
---
## Step 3: Apply Computational Thinking Skills
| Sub-Problem | CT Skill | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Some students take too long to decide what to order. | Decomposition | Create a digital menu showing food names and prices so students can choose before reaching the cashier. |
| The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change. | Algorithm | Create a program that adds the prices of selected items and automatically calculates the change. |
| There is no system to track which food items are running out. | Pattern Recognition | Track how many of each food item are sold and identify which items are frequently running out. |

---
## Step 4: Algorithmic Solution
### Selected Sub-Problem
The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
### Pseudocode
START

Display food menu and prices
Ask student to select food
Ask student for quantity
Calculate:
(tab) Total = price x quantity
Display total amount
Ask student for payment
Calculate:
(tab) Change = payment - total
Display total amount
Display change

END
